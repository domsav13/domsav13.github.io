Hello! I'm Dominic, an aspiring engineer and graduate student of mechanical engineering and robotics from Mechanicsburg, PA. My current studies focus on machine learning and electromechanical controls, vision and perception, mechanical design, and robotic systems. With the near-exponential deployment of robots in human-centered spaces, I care less about data-driven design and more about developing the tools, backed by first principles and mathematical rigor, which will guarantee stability, safety, and reliability under uncertainty as our everyday lives become more and more aided by robotics. 

In 2025, I received my bachelor's degree (B.S.) from George Washington University with a major in mechanical engineering, concentration in aerospace engineering, and minor in mathematics. Since starting my degree, I have had several fulfilling experiences (highlighted in my [cv](https://domsav13.github.io/cv/)) as well as numerous exploratory projects that I've compiled in my ongoing [engineering portfolio](https://domsav13.github.io/blog/). I strive to bring a process-driven approach to my work as I am motivated by how theory translates into practice, even when that translation reveals its limits. 

Receiving an education in the nation's capital has had a tremendous effect on the definition of my personal beliefs. Of these, two values in particular stand out as someone still working in the academic space: accessibility and authenticity. I am optimistic that world-class education will one day be available at a fraction of today's cost, freeing the next generation of changemakers from barriers imposed by inequity rather than potential. Secondly, I believe authenticity is vital in not only supporting diversity and inclusion, but also in bringing about impactful change through taking risks and challenging tradition. At the end of the day, education either helps integrate individuals into the logic of the present or it becomes the practice of freedom, where one deals critically and creatively with reality and discovers how to participate in their unique vision of the world.
