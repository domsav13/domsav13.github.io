Hello! I'm Dominic, an aspiring engineer from Mechanicsburg, PA. My current studies focus on **machine learning and control, vision and perception, mechanical design, and robotic systems**. With the near-exponential deployment of robots in human-centered spaces, I care less about data-driven design and more about developing the tools, backed by first principles and mathematical rigor, which will guarantee safety and reliability as our everyday lives become more and more aided by robotics. 

In 2025, I received my bachelor's degree from George Washington University with a major in mechanical engineering, concentration in aerospace engineering, and minor in mathematics. Since starting my degree, I have had several fulfilling [experiences](https://domsav13.github.io/cv/) as well as numerous exploratory projects that I've compiled in my ***ongoing [engineering portfolio](https://domsav13.github.io/blog/)***. I strive to bring a process-driven approach to my work as I am motivated by how theory translates into practice, even when that translation reveals its limits. 

Receiving an education in the nation’s capital has deeply shaped my values, particularly around accessibility and authenticity in academia. I am optimistic that world-class education will one day be available at a fraction of today’s cost, freeing future changemakers from barriers rooted in inequity rather than potential. Ultimately, I believe education either helps integrate individuals into the logic of the present or it becomes the practice of freedom, where one deals critically and creatively with reality, challenges tradition, and discovers how to participate in their unique vision of the world.
